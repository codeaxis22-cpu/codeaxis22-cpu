Developer Profile

Building high-octane digital experiences

Who We Are

CodeXis is an independent game development studio dedicated to crafting immersive, atmospheric, and high-quality digital experiences. We focus on building games for both PC and mobile platforms, designing experiences that challenge reflexes, reward player coordination, and push the boundaries of different genres—including psychological horror, deep survival systems, detailed simulations, and high-octane action games.

Our Mission & Target

Our target is to deliver visually rich, atmospheric, and gameplay-focused titles that resonate with PC and mobile players alike. By focusing on deep systems, immersive sound design, and robust multi-platform responsiveness, we aim to bridge the gap between platforms and deliver premium games across all major genres.

Roadmap

Our flight path and future plans

Phase 1

Launch & Polish

Release of Endless Drive: Alien Escape on the Google Play Store. Fine-tuning accelerometer tilt sensitivities, balancing health-loss fire states, and ensuring optimal performance on a wide spectrum of Android devices.

Phase 2

The Garage Update

Introducing new cyber-highway biomes, daily login reward calendars, customizable car skins, and higher upgrade caps for Health, Base Speed, and Handling systems.

Phase 3

Competitive Drive

Integrating global high-score leaderboards via Google Play Services, launching automated achievements, and implementing daily goal/milestone challenges.

Phase 4

Next Horizon

Prototyping a brand new, highly anticipated sci-fi title. Extending developer assets and building cross-platform capabilities to support future iOS releases.
